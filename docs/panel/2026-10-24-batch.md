Checks the moderator ran rather than assumed:

- **Tap size.** `.tc-btn`, `.tc-chip`, `.tc-choice` and `.tc-icon-btn` all get `min-height` and `min-width` of `--tc-tap` (56px) in `src/styles/ui.css`. The Battle Plan cards are `.tc-chip`, so they meet it. Builder save slots are `min-width: 56px; min-height: 64px`. `e2e/phase2.spec.ts` measures a castle card at 56px or taller, and `e2e/computer.spec.ts` runs `smallTargets` on Setup with the Computer player card showing. Both pass.
- **Contrast** (WCAG formula on the token hex values). Picked Battle Plan card: white on Blue 700 (`#1a3fb5`) is 8.7:1. Unpicked card: Ink Deep on Parchment Warm is 15.6:1. Picked chip on a Setup card: Ink Deep on Ember is 10.3:1. All pass 4.5:1.
- **Reading level** (Flesch-Kincaid, rough on short lines). "High. Solid rock below." 2.9. "Middle. A low wall." 0. "Low. Blocks flat shots." below 0. "Alex, place your castle" 3.7. "It takes its own turns. You keep the screen." below 0. "Drag a brick. Tap Turn to spin it." below 0. "Not enough Scrap yet. You need 4 more." below 0. "Computer player is aiming" scores 9.6, but only because a four-word line holds the three-syllable word "computer". The robot face next to it carries the meaning for a pre-reader.
- **Pace arithmetic.** At 400 health, a direct hit is 25 and a Power Shot direct hit is 37.5. A perfect turn (2 hits and a Power Shot) is 87.5, so about 4.6 turns to win. With a wrong answer it is 50 a turn, so 8 turns. "Short" (`healthScale` 0.5) is about 2.3 perfect turns; "Long" (1.5) is about 7.

**Confused or bothered.**
- On the Drawbridge field the drawbridge is a wooden board floating in the sky over the castle, with no chains and no posts (`battle-drawbridge-bridge-1024x768.png`). "Why is the door flying?" The Future bridge truss on the same screen floats too.

- The Build Base button at the Camp is blue and sits on the Future side, but it opens Team Past first. A Team Past child might not tap it.
- At 400 health the bar barely moves after one hit. A 6-year-old wants to see the bar drop.

## The teacher (K to 5, cares that the question stays the heart)

**Likes.** Gate 3 holds with the Computer player. The e2e test plays a whole match: the child gets a covered reload card on every own turn and answers it (`answerReload`), and the computer's turn never shows a question card or a cover. Its "thinking" note never shows a number or a question mark (the test checks that). Its own answers use a separate random stream and leave the child's history and level nudges alone. A longer match (400 health) means more reload questions per match, which is more practice. Every child-facing line I checked in this batch reads at third grade or below.

**Confused or bothered.**
- The Computer player copies the child's grade and subject, which is fine, but a child alone has no adult reading along. For K and 1, the reload card still needs a reader, because read-aloud is gone (owner decision, 2026-10-23). That is not new, but the Computer player makes "playing alone" easy for a pre-reader who cannot read the card. The game does not block or shame them (Gate 2), but they will mostly guess.
- On Setup, the hint "Both mixes reading and math." stays under Subject even when Math is picked (`computer-player-setup-1024x768.png`). Older than this batch, but it reads like a wrong label.
- Build Base and the Forge are time away from questions. They do not skip or shrink the reload, so Gate 3 is not broken.
- No BLOCK on learning. My CHANGES on the Computer player is about K and 1, and it is a note, not a stop.

## Recommended changes, ranked

1. **Pace (e): make one hit still feel big at 400 health.** Before it ships, time a real Normal match with two players and with the Computer player on Medium. If it runs past about 10 minutes, consider Short as the default for K to 2. Check the HUD reads "400 / 400" on one line at 1024x768 (Regression 11 and `tests/ui/handoffReview.test.ts` nowrap). Owner: the balance branch.
2. **Pace (e): refresh the tumble lines.** Drop the near twins listed above and add at least 6 new kind, silly lines per team, since a 400-health match shows far more tumbles. Copy lint and `tests/game/bag.test.ts` still apply.
3. **Build Base (a): fix the 1024x768 header.** The third save slot runs under the Scrap box, and the saved-base thumbnail pokes out of slot 1. Add a geometry check to `e2e/base-builder.spec.ts` like the layout specs (no overlap between `bb-team-*`, slots and `bb-scrap`). Note another branch is editing `src/ui/baseBuilderScreen.ts` now.
4. **Build Base (a): be honest about what a built base does.** Until `BASE_IN_BATTLE` is on, either show a short line in the builder ("Your base shows in battles soon.") or show the built base behind the fighters as decoration. Andy's call which.
5. **Obstacle art (c): hang the drawbridge.** Draw two chains up to the castle (and posts for the Future bridge truss) as non-solid background art, behind the shape and clearly thinner, so it never looks like it blocks shells. This bends the "picture equals solid shape" rule for decoration only, so it needs Andy's nod.
6. **Obstacle art (c): keep the aim label off fighter A at Hill Fort.** Move the dial label or nudge balloon fighter A when the castle is high.
7. **Build Base (a): make the Camp button read as both teams.** A neutral color or a castle-and-dome icon on the button, so a Team Past child knows it is theirs too. The same is true of the Forge button on the Past side.
8. **Naming (b):** "Village Wall" and the "Wall" ability are both Team Past walls. Consider renaming the ability button to "Stone Wall" in full, or the place to "Village Gate". Small copy change, Andy's call.
9. **Computer player (d): a note for K and 1.** When the child's grade is K or 1 and Computer player is picked, show one line on Setup: "A grown-up can read the questions with you." No read-aloud is added.
10. **Computer player (d): one Chrono line for it at Victory**, cheeky and warm, never mean to the child.
11. **Setup:** hide or change "Both mixes reading and math." when Reading or Math is picked. Older than this batch.
